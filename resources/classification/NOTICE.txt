Cedarflake Ame classification vocabulary

English nouns, definitions and relations are derived from Princeton WordNet 3.0
through the OMW English WordNet 2.0 WN-LMF conversion.
https://github.com/omwn/omw-data/releases/tag/v2.0

Chinese noun labels are derived from Chinese Open WordNet 1.1.
https://github.com/bond-lab/cow/releases/tag/v1.1

The accompanying WORDNET-LICENSE and COW-LICENSE retain the copyright notices,
license terms and disclaimers. Source citations accompany both resources.

Ame selects noun concepts, joins available Chinese labels, builds bounded graph
entry points and relates lexical cues to separately authored image contexts.
This transformation does not imply endorsement by the original authors.
Lexical cues do not themselves authorize a category name or a classification.
